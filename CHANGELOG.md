# Changelog

## 1.0.2

- **The door sound no longer sticks.** On aircraft where two bay doors share one
  speaker, opening every bay at once left them restarting each other's clip, which
  came out as a looping fragment of the servo sound.

## 1.0.1

- All of an aircraft's weapon bays open, not only some of them.
- Aircraft that ignored the key entirely now respond, including several fighters.
- Landing gear doors are no longer touched, so the gear animation is left alone.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- A key opens and closes the weapon bay doors on the aircraft you are flying.
- The key is configurable, and there is a hold-instead-of-toggle option.
